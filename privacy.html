Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short
  • We only collect the details you choose to send us (name, phone number and your appointment request).
  • We use them only to reply to you and manage your booking. We never sell or rent them.
  • This website has no login and no passwords, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • The whole site is served over a secure, encrypted HTTPS connection.

1. Who we are

This website, blownglowsalon.com, belongs to Blow N Glow Salon, a family salon and bridal studio at 1st Floor, 52G, Durugam Road, Opp. Old LIC, Raja Nagar, Kallakurichi, Tamil Nadu 606202 (GST: 33CKBPS6006F2ZF). In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Blow N Glow Salon.

2. Information we collect

When you fill in the enquiry / booking form: your name, phone number, the service you are interested in, your preferred date and time, and any message you type.

When you call, WhatsApp, email or message us on Instagram or Facebook: your phone number, name or profile name, and whatever you choose to share in the conversation.

Basic technical data: like every website, our hosting provider and the font and code libraries the page loads (Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs) may automatically record standard details such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the visit, for security and to deliver the page. We do not use this to identify you.

We do not ask for or store passwords, bank or card details, Aadhaar or any other government ID through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and confirm, change or remind you about appointments.
  • To understand which service, package or bridal look you need and give you an accurate quote.
  • To keep a simple record of enquiries so that we can follow up properly.

We will not send you promotional messages unless you have asked us to, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

4. Where your information is stored

Enquiry form entries are saved to a private Google Sheet in the salon's own Google account, which only the salon owner and authorised staff can open. WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, phone and email conversations stay in those apps, under their own privacy policies.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal details. We share them only:

  • with the service providers that run this website and our tools (for example GitHub for hosting and Google for the enquiry sheet), only as needed for them to work; or
  • if the law, a court or a government authority requires it.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and it does not show third-party ads. The links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps open those services, which have their own cookie and privacy policies.

7. How we keep your information safe

  • The website is served only over HTTPS, using a free SSL/TLS certificate issued by Let's Encrypt through GitHub Pages, so data sent from the form is encrypted in transit.
  • The site is a static website with no login, no admin panel and no database on the site itself, which leaves very little that can be attacked.
  • Browser security rules (a Content Security Policy) only allow the page to load code from trusted sources, and other websites cannot load our pages inside their own pages.
  • Access to the website, the domain and the enquiry sheet is limited to the owner and is protected by strong passwords and two-step verification.

No website can promise to be completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your details.

8. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to serve you and keep basic business records, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

9. Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal details we hold about you;
  • correct or update them;
  • delete them; or
  • stop contacting you.

Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

Our services are for the whole family, but bookings for children should be made by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect details directly from children through this website.

11. Photos, videos and content

All photos, videos, text, the logo and the design on this website belong to Blow N Glow Salon or are used with permission. They may not be copied, downloaded, reused or posted elsewhere without our written permission. Client photos are shown only with the client's consent. If you appear in a photo and would like it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date at the top.
