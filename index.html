About us

The Blow N Glow story.

Blow N Glow® is a women-owned family salon and bridal studio in Kallakurichi, opposite Old LIC on Durugam Road. Hair, skin, nails, makeup and bridal work are all done under one roof, for women, men and children.

Every visit starts with a consultation. Our stylists look at your face shape, hair density and skin before they suggest a cut, colour or treatment, and they explain each step. Many of our clients have been coming to us for years.
